Do I need a licensed electrician for electrical work in NSW?

Yes. In NSW, electrical wiring work must be carried out by, or under the supervision of, a licensed electrician, for homes, businesses and industrial sites alike. Altech Electrical holds NSW licence 299501C, and every job is tested and completed to Australian Standards.

We're based in Loftus and service the whole Sutherland Shire: Sutherland, Miranda, Caringbah, Cronulla, Gymea, Kirrawee, Jannali, Como, Sylvania, Taren Point, Menai, Bangor, Engadine, Heathcote, Oyster Bay, Woolooware, Burraneer, Kurnell, Bundeena and nearby southern Sydney suburbs.

Can you upgrade my old switchboard?

Yes. If your home still has an old fuse box, ceramic fuses or circuits that trip when modern appliances run, we inspect the board, recommend the right upgrade and quote clearly before starting. Modern switchboards with safety switches protect your family and support renovations, air conditioning and high-load appliances.

Why does my safety switch keep tripping?

Common causes are a faulty appliance, moisture in a circuit, damaged wiring or an overloaded circuit. The safest path is a proper inspection: we test each circuit, isolate the exact cause and repair it so the tripping stops for good.

Do I need smoke alarms in my home?

Yes. NSW homes must have at least one working smoke alarm on each level, and Fire and Rescue NSW recommends additional alarms in bedrooms, living spaces and hallways. We install and test smoke alarms for owner-occupied homes, rentals and strata units.
